# Company Website - Content Strategy & SEO Optimization

Content architecture, copywriting, and technical/on-page SEO strategies designed for chautech.de to improve organic visibility and B2B lead capture.

---

### 🚀 Key Focus Areas & Achievements
- **B2B Content Strategy:** Formulated high-converting landing page copy and service descriptions aligned with AI solution offerings.
- **On-Page & Technical SEO:** Optimized meta titles, headers (H1-H3), keyword density, and internal linking structure for core target keywords.
- **Search Intent & Keyword Mapping:** Researched commercial and informational search intent to capture inbound B2B queries.

---

### 🛠 Tools Used
- **SEO & Keyword Research:** Ahrefs / SEMrush / Google Keyword Planner
- **Analytics & Webmaster:** Google Search Console, Google Analytics
- **CMS & Content Management:** WordPress / Webflow
